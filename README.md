# GHOST CTS-4 — CODE TO SURVIVE

**Created by Hailstones**

GHOST CTS-4 is a dark, action-packed browser game that brings together survival, intelligence, strategy, and Python programming. It follows the story of a mysterious former intelligence operative whose past catches up with him, forcing him into a fight where both his technical knowledge and survival skills will be tested.

## About Hailstones

Hailstones is a developer and digital creator passionate about building interactive experiences, exploring AI, and bringing creative ideas to life.

GHOST CTS-4 is part of that journey — a project that combines action, strategy, and programming into an interactive gaming experience.

## The Story Behind GHOST

Before the name GHOST became associated with one of the most difficult people to track, he was just a strange kid in school with an unusual interest in computers.

While other students were occupied with ordinary school activities, he spent his time experimenting with computer systems, discovering vulnerabilities, and carrying out small hacks. What started as curiosity gradually developed into a serious interest in technology and cybersecurity.

He was naturally quiet, observant, and unusually good at understanding how systems worked. He rarely needed to be the loudest person in a room. He preferred watching, learning, and figuring things out for himself.

However, life did not turn out the way he expected.

As he grew older, difficult experiences, betrayal, and personal disappointments changed his outlook. The curious young boy became increasingly distant from the people around him. He stopped trusting easily and learned to hide his emotions.

He didn't develop supernatural powers. Instead, he became cold, calculated, and difficult to understand. He learned to control his reactions and approach situations without allowing emotions to interfere with his decisions.

Eventually, his technical abilities opened the door to the world of intelligence operations.

## The NSA Incident

Ghost eventually became associated with the National Security Agency (NSA), where his knowledge of cybersecurity, intelligence systems, and digital operations placed him in a position of significant access.

But his story took a different direction when he hacked government financial systems and stole enormous amounts of government funds.

The incident triggered a major investigation. His actions attracted the attention of intelligence and law enforcement agencies, including the CIA and FBI.

They wanted answers, and more importantly, they wanted to find him.

But Ghost had disappeared.

Attempts to establish his identity and whereabouts led to dead ends. His digital footprint became difficult to follow, and investigators struggled to connect the available information to a reliable location.

He had already left the country.

People eventually started referring to him as **Ghost** because of his ability to disappear without leaving a trace that could reliably lead to him. He became a name associated with mystery, uncertainty, and an identity that seemed impossible to pin down.

He wasn't a ghost in the supernatural sense. He was simply someone nobody could seem to find.

## The Ghost Look

Ghost's appearance reflects the person he has become. He doesn't dress like an ordinary civilian, and he doesn't need to reveal his identity to make his presence known.

His signature appearance includes:

- A dark tactical combat suit.
- A black hood that casts a shadow over his face.
- A protective face covering that conceals his identity.
- Tactical gloves and reinforced combat boots.
- A military-style vest with equipment and utility compartments.
- Dark protective gear suited to the dangerous environments he operates in.

His appearance is simple, intimidating, and functional. It gives him a recognisable identity without revealing the person behind the mask.

## The Hunt

Ghost may have escaped the investigation, but his past has not disappeared.

The CIA and FBI are still looking for him. His actions have made him a subject of continued interest, and the mystery surrounding his disappearance remains unresolved.

However, the investigation is no longer the only problem he has to worry about.

A new threat emerges: **THE COMMANDER**.

The Commander represents a different kind of challenge. This is not simply another investigation or another attempt to trace Ghost. It is a confrontation that will push his abilities, intelligence, and survival skills to their limits.

Eventually, their paths will cross.

Whether Ghost can overcome the Commander or finally get caught is something the player will have to discover.

## The Game

**GHOST CTS-4: Code to Survive** is more than a traditional shooter. It combines action and survival gameplay with interactive Python programming challenges.

Ghost's technical background is reflected in the game's mechanics. Players must understand programming concepts, solve challenges, and use their knowledge to earn resources that can help them survive.

The game introduces concepts such as:

- Variables and data types.
- Lists and tuples.
- Sets and ranges.
- Conditional statements.
- Logical operations.
- Data manipulation.
- Programming problem-solving.

Players can complete challenges to earn ammunition, shields, bombs, and other abilities.

The idea is simple: your ability to think and solve problems matters just as much as your ability to survive the battlefield.

## Game Features

- **Action and survival:** Take on challenging missions and survive dangerous encounters.
- **Python-based challenges:** Apply programming concepts through interactive gameplay.
- **Programming-inspired weapons:** Explore weapons and ammunition mechanics connected to Python concepts.
- **AI-powered assistance:** Get help understanding programming challenges.
- **Voice features:** Experience additional voice interaction.
- **Mobile and desktop support:** Play with controls designed for different devices.
- **Progressive difficulty:** Face increasingly difficult missions and prepare for the final confrontation with THE COMMANDER.
